What was the hardest thing to build?+×

FlowConX. Classifying traffic you cannot decrypt means every signal has to come from timing and shape: packet sizes, inter arrival times, jitter, byte rates. Getting a contrastive objective to produce clean, well separated embeddings from that, across three datasets collected under completely different conditions, took far more iteration than the architecture did.
